# Pixel Kingdom Rumble — Support

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at https://github.com/marcompsci/Pixel-Kingdom-Rumble/issues and include your
iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. TestFlight testers can also take a screenshot in the game and tap
"Share Beta Feedback".

**Restoring a purchase:** open Shadow Contracts and tap RESTORE PURCHASE.

**Assist mode:** Settings > Assist mode gives extra health and slower guards if a level is too hard.
