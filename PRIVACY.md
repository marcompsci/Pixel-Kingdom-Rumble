# Pixel Kingdom Rumble — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Pixel Kingdom Rumble is a single-player game. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no ads.** The game has no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Save data (levels cleared, scores, settings) is stored only on your iPhone.
- **Game Center (optional).** If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your achievements and the Shadow Contracts
  score are sent to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we never receive your Apple ID or contact details.
- **In-app purchase.** The optional Shadow Contracts unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your
  payment details.
- **Children.** The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Questions or support: open an issue at https://github.com/marcompsci/Pixel-Kingdom-Rumble/issues
