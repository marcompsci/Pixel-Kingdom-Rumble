# Pixel Kingdom Rumble — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Pixel Kingdom Rumble does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. We run no servers of our own.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no ads.** The game has no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Save data (progress, scores, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your iPhone
  or iPad. Deleting the app deletes it.
- **Game Center (optional).** If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your achievements and best scores are sent to
  Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we never receive your Apple Account or contact details.
- **Online co-op (optional).** Online Last Stand uses Game Center matchmaking. While you play together, the game sends
  gameplay data (hero positions, actions, the round and score) and shows each player's Game Center nickname to the other
  players in that match. This goes through Apple's Game Center service directly between the players' devices; we do not
  receive or keep it. There is no text or voice chat.
- **Game Center friends (optional).** The leaderboards page can show where you rank among your Game Center friends. iOS
  asks your permission first; the friends list is read on your device to show the ranking and is never sent to us.
- **Photos (optional).** If you choose "Save Image" from the share sheet, the game adds that picture to your photo
  library. It never reads your photos.
- **In-app purchase.** The optional Shadow Contracts unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see
  your payment details.
- **Children.** The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Questions or support: open an issue at https://github.com/marcompsci/Pixel-Kingdom-Rumble/issues
