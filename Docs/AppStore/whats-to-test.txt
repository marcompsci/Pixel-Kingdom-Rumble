Thanks for testing Pixel Kingdom Rumble! This build leads with STORY QUEST (the first main-menu button).

New in this build:
- KINGDOM PLAZA: Story Quest now opens in a street you can walk. Its buildings lead to the four Story worlds and five
  party quests. The sign over each building shows your progress and roughly how many players are there right now
  (from Game Center; "--" when it can't tell, for example when you're not signed in).
- Five party quests against CPU rivals: ITEM RACE, BALLOON BATTLE, RING-OUT BRAWL, COIN RUSH and KING OF THE HILL,
  with item boxes (Dash Fizz, Bubble Shield, Seeker Star, Slick Puddle, Thunder Bell).
- QUICK GAME widget (iOS 17+): a tiny Story Quest stage on your Home Screen. Tap left/right to step, JUMP to jump.
  Clears pay Star Shards in the game; see Story Quest > STORY STATS.
- Live seasons: Story Quest dresses itself for the real date (autumn harvest now, winter snow from December).
- Story Stats page, time medals, menu and font clean-up.

Please try:
1. STORY QUEST: walk the plaza, enter each building, and come back with PLAZA. Any dead ends?
2. Each party quest once. Fair? Too long? Do the CPUs ever get stuck?
3. Add the QUICK GAME widget (touch and hold the Home Screen > + > Pixel Kingdom Rumble). Clear a stage, then open the
   game: do the Star Shards and stats show up?
4. With a friend: are the live counts on the plaza signs believable? (They are approximate.)
5. ZOMBIES and ONLINE CO-OP still work as before.

Known: the Shadow Contracts purchase uses the TestFlight sandbox (no real charge). Party quests are against the
computer, not online.
