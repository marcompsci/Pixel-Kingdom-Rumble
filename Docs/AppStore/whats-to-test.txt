Thanks for testing Pixel Kingdom Rumble! STORY QUEST is the first main-menu button.

New in this build:
- KINGDOM PLAZA: Story Quest opens in a street you can walk. Buildings lead to the four Story worlds and five party quests (Item Race, Balloon Battle, Ring-Out Brawl, Coin Rush, King of the Hill) against CPU rivals. Signs show roughly how many players are there (from Game Center; "--" when it can't tell).
- QUICK GAME widget (iOS 17+): a tiny Story Quest stage on your Home Screen.
- ARENA CLASH: ITEMS on/off, choose which hero the CPUs play, and a TRAINING LAB (the CPU can walk, jump or fight back; a combo counter tracks your hits). Hard CPUs now guard the ledge when you're off the stage.
- VERSUS LADDER: pick your fighter, tap LADDER and beat 3-5 rivals in a row, Easy up to Hard.
- ZOMBIES: the BARGAIN BELL power-up in Last Stand (box spins cost 10 for 20 s) and a FIELD GUIDE of every kind of the dead.
- STORY QUEST: a time-trial ghost of your best clear, FLAWLESS clears, a shard compass on replays, and Settings > STORY GAME SPEED (100-70%).
- A short welcome guide for new players, and the DAILY page now lists every daily in the game.
- NEW: CONTINUE and PROFILE on the main menu, HERO MASTERY (every hero levels up in every mode), a daily login gift, WHAT'S NEW, Versus head-to-head records, best combos in Arena results, training RESET, Item Race medals and party bests.
- Settings > ASSISTS: comfort preset, Story speed, an invincible assist (no records) and RESET HINTS & GUIDES. Story levels show where you fell last time.

Please try:
1. STORY QUEST: walk the plaza, enter buildings, replay a cleared level (ghost + compass).
2. ARENA CLASH with ITEMS ON, and TRAINING with each CPU option.
3. VERSUS > pick a fighter > LADDER. Is the climb fair?
4. ZOMBIES > FIELD GUIDE, then a Last Stand run. Did you see the Bargain Bell?
5. Settings > STORY GAME SPEED at 70%: does it feel easier, and is anything broken?
6. Add the QUICK GAME widget and clear a stage.
7. Main menu > PROFILE: do the numbers look right? Does CONTINUE take you back to where you were?

Known: the Shadow Contracts purchase uses the TestFlight sandbox (no real charge). Party quests and the ladder are against the computer, not online.
