Thanks for testing Pixel Kingdom Rumble! STORY QUEST is the first main-menu button.

New in this build (six rounds of improvements):
- BADGES: 36 badges with progress. PROFILE > BADGES lists them all. Every 5 badges pays 25 Star Shards, and NEXT BADGE on the menu shows the closest one.
- STORY QUEST: a one-heart BRAVE challenge (Settings > ASSISTS), checkpoint splits and shard counts, PERFECT clears, MEDAL UP and rank-up callouts, CLOSE CALL, ON A ROLL, tries per level, world completion %, and a SHARE button on Story Stats.
- PAUSE: BACK TO CHECKPOINT and KINGDOM PLAZA. RESTART and leaving a level now need two taps so a stray tap won't cost a run.
- VERSUS: best of 5, RANDOM MATCHUP, win streaks, FIRST WIN, REVENGE and GRUDGE MATCH callouts, and your record with each hero.
- ARENA CLASH: MVP on the results, best KOs and combo records, KO milestones.
- ZOMBIES: accuracy, average round, YOUR BEST and NEW BEST callouts, kill milestones, last run and weeklies beaten.
- HERO SELECT: mastery, Versus record, your FAVORITE and a RANDOM button.
- SHOP: what you can afford now and what you're saving for.
- PROFILE: playing since, days played, week streak, fastest clear, Arena and Versus records, ABOUT page.
- Menu: greeting, tip of the day (tap for more), weekly recap, THIS SESSION recap, welcome-back gift.
- Settings: Longer messages (Display), Quiet celebrations and Menu tips & goals (Assists), optional hourly break reminder.

Please try:
1. Turn on BRAVE, clear a Story level, then replay it. Do the splits, MEDAL UP and tries look right?
2. PROFILE > BADGES: page through. Do the progress numbers match what you've done?
3. Pause in a level and tap RESTART once. Does it ask you to tap again?
4. VERSUS: win 3 in a row, then lose. Did the streak messages show?
5. ARENA CLASH: is the MVP tag on the right fighter?
6. Hero select > RANDOM, and the Shop's "Saving for" line.
7. Too many pop-ups? Try Settings > ASSISTS > Quiet celebrations and tell us if it helps.

Nothing new is tracked: every record stays in the save on your device.
Known: the Shadow Contracts purchase uses the TestFlight sandbox (no real charge). Party quests and the ladder are against the computer, not online.
