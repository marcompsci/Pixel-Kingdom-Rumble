Thanks for testing Pixel Kingdom Rumble! This build leads with ZOMBIES (the first main-menu button).

New in this build:
- GRAVEMOOR PARK, a fourth Last Stand map, and ZOMBIE LAND CHAPTER 4: COLD HARBOR (after the Curator).
- New dead: SPLITTERS (fall into two halves) and BURROWERS (tunnel under the street; step away when you see "!").
- THE KINDLER, a new wonder weapon in the mystery box: the zombie it hits fights the others for a while.
- OVERCHARGE (take a forged gun through the forge again), weapon finishes (LOCKER > ARMORY) and PRESTIGE at rank 30.
- Online co-op: a lobby list, START NOW, a connection readout, and dropped players get their hero back.
- Settings > DISPLAY & ACCESS: graphics (Auto / High / Battery saver), Show FPS, colour vision, reduced motion,
  left-handed controls, bigger touch targets, sound cues on screen.
- New music for each zombie map and for boss fights.

Please try:
1. ZOMBIES > LAST STAND on Gravemoor Park. Light the three lanterns. Too hard? Too easy?
2. ZOMBIE LAND: if you've finished chapter 3, take the road by the rail depot to Cold Harbor.
3. With a friend on TestFlight: ONLINE CO-OP. Does it feel smooth? What happens if one of you loses signal?
4. Settings > DISPLAY & ACCESS: anything that doesn't do what it says?
5. Long sessions: does the phone get hot or the game slow down? (Show FPS helps.)

Known: the Shadow Contracts purchase uses the TestFlight sandbox (no real charge).
