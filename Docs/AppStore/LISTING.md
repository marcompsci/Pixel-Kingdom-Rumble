### Description

Hold the line against the dead, sneak across rooftops and brawl with an original pixel-art cast, all in one game built for iPhone.

ZOMBIES, ALL YEAR
Zombies sit at the top of the main menu and never leave.
- LAST STAND: round-based survival on two maps, Hollow Block and Salt Pier. Earn points for every hit and kill, buy wall guns, clear debris to open more of the map, drink perks, spin the mystery box for one of two wonder weapons and hollow-forge your favourite gun.
- PLAY TOGETHER: up to four players online through Game Center, or bring an AI squad that covers you, revives you and grabs better guns as the rounds climb.
- ENDLESS, RAID OR HARDCORE: chase your best round, win a 10-round Raid, or take on tougher dead for bigger rewards.
- WEEKLY CHALLENGE: a new map, mode and pair of mutators every Monday, with a reward for reaching the goal.
- SECRETS: every map hides an Easter egg that wakes a hidden boss.
- ZOMBIE LAND: an open quarantined city to explore by car, bike or on foot. Fourteen missions, ten Graystone shards and a mystery to solve before you face the First Turned. Then cross the causeway for Chapter 2 on Blackwater Isle.
- SURVIVOR RANK: one career across every zombie mode, with rewards at every rank. Nothing is for sale.

STORY QUEST
Hand-built levels across several worlds. Bound off spring pads, climb vines, dodge crushers and lasers, ride drifting plates over spike pits and face the bosses at the end of each world.

STEALTH
Guards carry a visible sight cone that turns from pale to amber to red as they notice you. Hide, strike from behind for a silent takedown, steal the target and escape unseen.

SHADOW CONTRACTS
A mission mode for the Order of the Hidden Gear, with secret contracts and high scores. The first contract is free; one purchase unlocks the full set.

VERSUS AND ARENA
Pick from a roster of original fighters for one-on-one Versus duels or a free-for-all Arena Clash.

MADE FOR TOUCH
A floating stick and big buttons you can move and resize. High contrast mode, screen shake toggle, haptics and an Assist mode.

GAME CENTER
Achievements and leaderboards, including your best Last Stand rounds and Survivor Rank.

No ads. No accounts. Your progress stays on your device.

## Description

Run, climb, sneak and fight across the floating Sunspire Isles in an original pixel-art adventure built for iPhone.

STORY QUEST
Eight hand-built levels across two worlds. Bound off spring pads, climb vines, ride drifting plates over spike pits and bump shard crates from below. Face the Clockwork Warden, then slip across the rooftops of the Brasslight Quarter and climb into the icy Frostgear Peaks.

STEALTH
Guards carry a visible sight cone that turns from pale to amber to red as they notice you. Duck into hay bales, strike from behind for a silent takedown, steal the target item and escape unseen. Every stealth run is graded GHOST, SHADOW, AGENT or BRAWLER.

SHADOW CONTRACTS
A mission mode for the Order of the Hidden Gear. Take down marked targets, steal relics of the first kings and chase high scores. Cipher scrolls hidden in the maps reveal secret contracts. The first contract is free; one purchase unlocks the full set of eight.

VERSUS AND ARENA
Pick from a roster of original fighters for one-on-one Versus duels or a free-for-all Arena Clash against CPU rivals.

MADE FOR TOUCH
A floating stick and big buttons you can move and resize. High contrast mode, screen shake toggle, haptics and an Assist mode with extra health and slower guards.

GAME CENTER
Ten achievements and a Shadow Contracts leaderboard.

No ads. No accounts. Your progress stays on your device.

## Notes for App Review

Thank you for reviewing Pixel Kingdom Rumble.

- No account or sign-in is needed.
- In-app purchase "Shadow Contracts" (non-consumable, $3.99): Main menu > SHADOW CONTRACTS. The first contract (First Light) is free; the purchase unlocks the rest. RESTORE PURCHASE is on the same screen.
- Game Center: the game signs in at launch; Main menu > GAME CENTER opens achievements and the leaderboard.
- Controls: left side is a floating stick; JUMP / ATK / SPL / DODGE buttons on the right. Settings > Assist mode makes levels easier if needed.
- Gameplay screens are landscape; menus are portrait.
