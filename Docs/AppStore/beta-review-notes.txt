No sign-in needed. STORY QUEST is the first main-menu button and opens the Kingdom Plaza: walk left/right and press ENTER at a door for a Story world or a party quest (all against CPU rivals). Live player counts on the signs come from Game Center and show "--" when signed out. The Quick Game widget (iOS 17+) is in the Home Screen widget gallery. Seasonal looks switch on by date and are all in this build. ZOMBIES is the second button; online co-op needs a second Game Center account. Shadow Contracts (Main Menu > SHADOW CONTRACTS) is the only purchase; RESTORE PURCHASE is on the same screen. New: Arena Clash items, CPU hero picks and a training lab; a Versus ladder (VERSUS > pick a fighter > LADDER); a zombie Field Guide (ZOMBIES > FIELD GUIDE); Story time-trial ghosts and a slower game speed option (Settings). The app collects no data.
