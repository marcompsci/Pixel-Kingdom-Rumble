# Pixel art (HD pass, October 2026)

All art is original and generated by code in `Tools/art/` (Python + Pillow). Re-run any time:

```
python3 Tools/art/build_all.py                 # everything
python3 Tools/art/build_all.py fighters tiles  # just some steps: fighters props tiles backgrounds fx
```

## Style
- 16-bit-style pixel art at **32 px per world unit** (2x the old placeholders, same world sizes).
- Directional light from the top-left, **hue-shifted ramps** (shadows lean blue/violet, lights lean warm),
  dark separation lines between overlapping parts, a 1 px tinted outline and a soft rim light.
- HD layer on top: bloom, vignette and a light colour grade (URP volume `Settings/Rendering/PKR_PostFX`),
  glowing pickups (`GlowHalo`), soft ground shadows under every fighter, a runtime sky gradient per level.

## Fighters (`Art/Pixel/Fighters/<id>/`)
80x48 frames, feet at the bottom centre, facing right. Clips: idle 4, run 6, jump 1, fall 1, attack 3
(wind-up, strike with the weapon arc, follow-through), special 2 (charge, release with a burst), hurt 1.
`FighterVisual` picks the frame from movement / attack state; procedural squash and stretch stay on top.
The menu sprite (`ph_<id>.png`) is a 32x48 crop of idle frame 0.

Ground is a tiled body plus a one-unit surface cap on every column that is open above (`PixelArt.Ground`).
Backdrops: `SkyGradient` (from the level's sky colour) + `ParallaxLayer` far / clouds / mid / near, each four
copies that leapfrog so any level width works.

## Enemies and bosses (`Art/Pixel/Enemies/<name>/`, `Art/Pixel/Bosses/`)
Same canvas and feet line as each enemy's single sprite. Walkers: idle 2, run (walk) 4, hurt. Flyers: a 4-frame wing
beat / propeller loop (jump = fall = air). The Spring Tick coils while idle and stretches in the air. Guards walk with a
short patrol stride. `PixelArt.ApplyEnemy` fills `EnemyDefinition.frames` (folder = id without `enemy_`);
`FighterVisual.SetFrames` plays them. The Warden / Sovereign face has five states (idle 0/1, angry during attacks,
dazed while the core is open, hurt) plus a hover, an attack shudder and a glowing core.

## Touch controls and menus (`Resources/PKR/UI/`)
- `Tools/art/controls.py`: original dark bevelled round buttons with white icons (jump, attack, special, dodge),
  a round D-pad base with a raised cross, a dotted knob, a direction glow and square HUD buttons (pause, sound).
  Settings has **D-pad (8-way)**: the stick snaps to eight directions at full strength.
- `Tools/art/ui.py`: 9-slice pixel panels and chunky press-down buttons, and **PKR Pixel**, an original 5x7
  TrueType display font (titles and buttons).
- The title screen shows six heroes from the roster idling and showing off moves.

## Movement extras (October 2026)
- **Double jump** for every hero (`PlatformerMotor2D.AirJumpAllowance`: at least one mid-air jump; heroes with
  more keep them). A front flip, sparkles and its own sound.
- **Rescue jetpack** (Story and Contracts, `JetpackRescue`): hold **JET** (keyboard F / O, gamepad LB) to fly on a
  1.4 s tank that refills on the ground; the ring around the button shows fuel. If the hero is stuck inside
  geometry or hung motionless by a glitch, JET warps them back to the last safe ground (the button pulses).
- On-screen controls are **65% opaque** by default (Settings > Control opacity) and the left stick floats: it
  appears under the thumb and slides along if the thumb goes past its rim. Existing players are moved to these
  defaults once (settings version 2).

## Sharing
- Arena results: **SHARE** builds a Wordle-style card (`Core/Share/ShareCard.cs`): Daily number, twist, placement,
  KO and fall squares, streak, points, tier and the TestFlight link.
- Pause menu: **PHOTO MODE** freezes the game, hides the HUD, adds an optional name banner and shares a PNG.
- iOS share sheet: `Plugins/iOS/PKRShare.mm`; Info.plist gets `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` for "Save Image".
