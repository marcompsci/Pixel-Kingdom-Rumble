# Pixel Kingdom Rumble: 30 improvements, round 10

All content is original. No new tracking, analytics or data collection: everything is kept in the local save.

## Shadow Contracts
1. **Tries** are counted for each contract.
2. **Briefing** shows YOUR BEST score and how many tries.
3. **Clear screen**: your score vs your best ("+150 over your best").
4. **SHADOW AGENT** badge (a score on 10 contracts).

## Story Quest
5. **Clear screen**: hero mastery XP earned and your mastery level.
6. **LAST HEART!** hint when you drop to one heart.
7. **ALL N SECRETS FOUND!** banner.
8. **Pause**: FLAWLESS SO FAR while you haven't been hit.
9. **Level select**: LAST PLAYED tag.
10. **Level select**: how many NEW levels a world has.

## Versus
11. **Perfect rounds** (won without losing a heart) are counted.
12. **FLAWLESS FIGHTER** badge (5 perfect rounds).
13. **Results**: mastery XP earned.
14. **Profile**: perfect rounds.
15. **Badges are checked** right after each match.

## Arena Clash
16. **FIRST ARENA CLASH WIN!** cheer.
17. **Training lab record**: your best combo is saved across sessions.
18. **Lab HUD** shows BEST (this session) and RECORD (all time).
19. **NEW LAB RECORD** flash for 5+ hit records.

## Zombies
20. **Game over**: time survived.
21. **Longest run** record ("(LONGEST!)").
22. **Zombies menu**: your longest Last Stand run.
23. **Profile**: longest run.

## Meta
24. **Titles**: SQUIRE, ADVENTURER, KNIGHT, HERO, LEGEND and MYTH, by badge count.
25. **Profile**: your title and how many badges until the next one.
26. **Greeting** uses your title ("Good evening, KNIGHT!").
27. **Today's play time** in the greeting ("You've played 25m today.").
28. **NIGHT OWL** and **EARLY BIRD** badges (40 in total).
29. **ALL BADGES**: TO EARN filter.
30. **Profile share** includes your title. Also 4 new tips (32 in total) and the What's New 1.7 card.
