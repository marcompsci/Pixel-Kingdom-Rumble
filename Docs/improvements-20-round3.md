# Pixel Kingdom Rumble: 20 improvements, round 3 (20 Nov 2026)

These are for the 1.1 update. Version 1.0 (build 2133) is in App Store review.

## The whole game
1. **CONTINUE.** The main menu's top button returns you to the last thing you played: a Story level, Kingdom Plaza, Arena Clash, Versus or Zombies.
2. **PROFILE.** A new main-menu page with your whole game in one place. It shows time played, hero mastery and Story (levels cleared, FLAWLESS clears, ghosts). It also covers Arena/Versus (rating record, ladder, your toughest rival), party quests, zombies (kills, best round, Field Guide) and your daily-gift streak.
3. **Play time.** Time played is counted per mode on the device for PROFILE. It saves every minute and when the app goes to the background.
4. **Hero mastery.** Every hero earns XP in every mode: Story clears, Arena/Versus/party wins and matches, Last Stand rounds and a Versus ladder clear.
   - There are 10 levels, from ROOKIE to LEGEND.
   - Levels 5 and 10 pay 100 and 300 Star Shards, once each.
   - A message appears when a hero levels up.
5. **Daily login gift.** The first time you open the game each day you get Star Shards: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, then 60 on a 7-day cycle. Missing a day restarts the streak.
6. **WHAT'S NEW.** A one-time card per update for returning players. New players get the welcome guide instead.
7. **RESET HINTS & GUIDES** (Settings > ASSISTS). The first-time hints, the party how-to-play cards and the welcome guide all come back.
8. **Comfort preset** (Settings > ASSISTS). One tap turns on Assist mode, 80% Story speed, strong aim assist and bigger touch targets.

## Arena Clash & Versus
9. **Best combos on the results screen.** Each fighter's best combo, counted for whoever landed the hits.
10. **Training lab RESET.** Puts everyone back at their start spot, refills the dummies' Guard Pips and clears the counter.
11. **Head-to-head records.** Your wins and losses against each CPU fighter, shown when you pick the CPU on the Versus screen.
12. **Mastery on the Versus screen.** Picking your fighter shows their mastery level and the XP to the next level.

## Story Quest
13. **Invincible assist** (Settings > ASSISTS). Hits still knock you back, but you lose no hearts; pits still send you back. Clears count but set no records, and they can't be FLAWLESS.
14. **Beat your ghost.** The results screen shows "BEAT YOUR GHOST BY 1.2s!" when your new best clear beats the ghost you raced.
15. **Level select badges.** FLAWLESS and GHOST appear on cleared levels.
16. **Fall markers.** Faint red arrows show where you fell on earlier tries, up to the last 8 per level. A flawless, no-fall clear clears them.

## Party quests
17. **Personal bests.** Each building's sign shows your best: Item Race time and medal, Coin Rush coins, King of the Hill crown seconds, and balloons left on a Balloon Battle win.
18. **Item Race medals.** GOLD at 70 s or less, SILVER at 85 s, BRONZE at 100 s. The medal shows on the results and on the sign.

## Settings, sharing and tests
19. **ASSISTS page and profile sharing.** Settings has a third page, ASSISTS, with the comfort preset, Story speed, invincible, ghost, Assist mode and reset hints. PROFILE has a SHARE button.
20. **Tests.**
    - 9 engine-free tests: play time, the login gift, mastery, head-to-head, fall memory, WHAT'S NEW, party bests and medals, the comfort preset and reset, and cleaning bad save data.
    - 5 PlayMode tests: Story continue / invincible / falls, main menu CONTINUE and PROFILE, WHAT'S NEW, the ASSISTS page, and training RESET.
