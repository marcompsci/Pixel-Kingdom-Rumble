# Pixel Kingdom Rumble: 25 improvements, round 8

All content is original. No new tracking, analytics or data collection: everything is kept in the local save.

## Arena Clash
1. **Career record**: matches, wins and KOs from every Arena Clash match (not Training or party quests).
2. **MVP** tag on the results for the fighter with the most KOs (no tag on a tie).
3. **New bests**: "NEW BEST: 5 KOs IN A MATCH!" and "NEW BEST COMBO: 6!" on the results.
4. **Career line** on the results when there's no new best.
5. **Profile**: Arena Clash record, total KOs, best KOs and best combo.
6. **KO milestones**: a toast at every 100 lifetime Arena KOs.
7. **KNOCKOUT ARTIST** badge (250 KOs).
8. **COMBO KING** badge (an 8-hit combo).
9. **CHAMPION** badge (50 Arena wins).

## Party quests
10. **PARTY ANIMAL** badge for playing all five party quests.
11. **Win milestones**: first win, then 5, 10, 25, 50… in each party quest.
12. **Profile**: your favorite party quest.

## Meta and settings
22. **Profile**: your latest 3 badges (and how many more you have).
