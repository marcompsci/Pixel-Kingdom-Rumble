# Pixel Kingdom Rumble: 20 improvements, round 4

All content is original. No new tracking, analytics or data collection: everything below is stored in the local save only.

## Story Quest
1. **BRAVE challenge**: an optional one-heart mode (Settings > ASSISTS). Clearing a level BRAVE pays +30 shards once and shows a BRAVE badge on level select. It doesn't count when invincible is on.
2. **Checkpoint splits**: your best run's checkpoint times are kept. Each checkpoint shows how far ahead or behind you are ("CHECKPOINT 2  -1.4s AHEAD!").
3. **Best line on the intro card**: the PAR card also shows your BEST time and the next medal target.
4. **Secret sense**: once every shard is found, the compass points at secret areas you haven't found yet.
5. **Pause: BACK TO CHECKPOINT** in Story levels.
6. **Pause: KINGDOM PLAZA** in Story levels.
7. **Fair FLAWLESS**: a flawless clear no longer counts while invincible.

## Versus and Arena
8. **Best of 5** option (ROUNDS: BEST OF 3/5) on Versus select.
9. **RANDOM MATCHUP**: one tap picks two random heroes and starts the fight.
10. **Rival callout**: a "VS <NAME>" toast with your head-to-head record against that hero.
11. **Arena setup remembered**: your last custom Arena setup is saved across launches.

## Zombies
12. **Last Stand accuracy**: the game-over card shows your shot accuracy.
13. **Run history**: your last 5 runs per map, with the average round on the map buttons ("best round 14 · avg 9").
14. **Field guide count** on the button (FIELD GUIDE 9/14).

## Profile and meta
15. **12 badges**: first clear, ten clears, flawless x5, brave, party x10, ladder, rated wins, 500 zombies, round 20, field guide, mastery, 7-day streak. A toast pops when you earn one, and they're listed in the profile.
16. **PROFILE · NEW** on the main menu when there's something new to see.
17. **Gift calendar**: ●●●○○○○ shows where you are in the 7-day gift cycle.
18. **Weekly recap**: on the first launch of a new week, a card sums up last week (levels, zombies, wins, time played).
19. **Tip of the day** on the main menu (12 rotating tips).
20. **Break reminder** (optional, off by default): a friendly toast after each hour of play.
