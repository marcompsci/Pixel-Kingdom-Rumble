## 001: "16 updates, never pressed Play" (2026-10-08)

**Where it is:** a whole game written with Claude over three days: four heroes (including Rex Rollo, a
roller-skating lizard), a boss, a level select, a shop and a Codex. About 10,000 lines of C#, 145 automated
tests passing… and it has never been opened in Unity. Today that changes: feature freeze until it runs on my iPhone.

**Honest problem:** I kept saying "next phase" instead of testing. Classic.
