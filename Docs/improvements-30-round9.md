# Pixel Kingdom Rumble: 30 improvements, round 9

All content is original. No new tracking, analytics or data collection: everything is kept in the local save.

## Hero select
1. **Mastery** level and title under a ready hero.
2. **Versus record** with that hero.
3. **★ FAVORITE** tag on your most-played hero.
4. **RANDOM** button (picks a different hero you can play).

## Shop
5. **SHOP · N** on the main menu when you can afford something.
6. **"You can afford N upgrades right now"** in the shop.
7. **"Saving for"**: the cheapest upgrade you can't buy yet, and how many shards to go.
8. **SHOPPER** badge (5 purchases).

## Codex
9. **Completion %** next to FOUND x/y.
10. **LOREKEEPER** badge (30 entries).

## Story Quest
11. **Intro card** shows TRY N and BRAVE.
12. **A helping hand**: after 3 falls in one run, a one-time pointer to the ASSISTS (not shown if you already use them).
13. **Pause** shows BRAVE during a BRAVE run.
14. **Story Stats SHARE** button (clears, golds, PERFECTs, best-times total).

## Versus
15. **Your overall record** (and streak) on Versus select.
16. **GRUDGE MATCH** callout when a rival leads you by 3 or more.
17. **Profile**: the rival you've beaten most.

## Zombies
18. **Best accuracy** on the Last Stand button.
19. **Round 1** shows your average round on the map, next to YOUR BEST.

## Badges and meta
20. **ALL BADGES** page: every badge with progress, 9 to a page.
21. **Profile BADGES** button opens it.
22. **Week streak**: how many weeks in a row you've played.
23. **SEASONED** badge (4 weeks in a row).
24. **Profile**: current and best week streak.
25. **Pause > MAIN MENU / ZOMBIES MENU** needs a second tap during a level (after the first 3 seconds).
26. **Pause > RESTART LEVEL** needs a second tap in the same way.
27. **Daily challenges** page shows how many you've done in total.
28. **HERO COLLECTOR** badge (6 heroes unlocked). 36 badges in total.
29. **4 new tips** (28 in total).
30. **What's New 1.6** card, and new TestFlight What to Test notes covering rounds 4-9.
