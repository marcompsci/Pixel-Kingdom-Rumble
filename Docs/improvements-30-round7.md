All content is original. No new tracking, analytics or data collection: everything is kept in the local save.

## Story Quest
1. **PERFECT clears**: S rank, FLAWLESS, and every shard and secret. The first one on each level pays +15 Star Shards. Not counted while invincible or at a slower game speed.
2. **Clear screen PERFECT row**.
3. **PERFECT tag** on level select cards.
4. **MEDAL UP: GOLD!** when a clear earns a better medal than your old best did.
5. **Next medal target** on the clear screen ("GOLD: 3.2s faster").
6. **Rank UP FROM A!** next to the rank when you beat your old best rank.
7. **World 100%**: a one-time celebration toast when a world's levels, shards and secrets are all done.
8. **TOUGHEST tag** on the level you've started most.
9. **Close calls** (clears on your last heart) are counted.
10. **Story Stats**: PERFECT and close-call counts.
